Little Moments,
Big Memories ❤️

Hum ab tak kitni hi jagahon par saath ghoom chuke hain… Shaheed Park, mele mein saath masti, jungle mein ghoomna… aur woh hiran dekhne ke chakkar mein hum dono ka gir jaana 😭❤️.

Aisi na jaane kitni chhoti-badi memories hain jo aaj bhi mujhe yaad hain.

Kabhi-kabhi main un sab moments ko bahut miss karta hoon. Woh random plans, woh hasi-mazaak, woh saath mein ghoomna… shayad jagah se zyada important hamesha tumhara saath tha.

Aur haan… tumne mujhe badminton khelna sikhaya 🏸❤️ aur “machhli banne ki training” bhi di 😂.

Shayad tumhe ye sab normal lage, but mere liye ye sab meri favourite memories ka part hain. 🌷
